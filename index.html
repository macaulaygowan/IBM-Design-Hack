      🌍 UN SDG Target 12.8 — What It Means

      The global framework behind this app

      SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production is one of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals adopted by all UN member states in 2015 as part of the 2030 Agenda.

      Target 12.8 specifically calls for ensuring that "by 2030, people everywhere have the relevant information and awareness for sustainable development and lifestyles in harmony with nature."

      CommonGround operationalises this by making sustainability data visible, meaningful, and community-anchored — countering the "my actions don't matter" fatigue that derails individual participation. When you see your neighbour saving 50 kg CO₂ by cycling, and the whole street collectively saving 2 tonnes, the individual action gains context and power.

      📐 CO₂ Calculation Methodology

      How we estimate your impact
      • Cycling 1 km vs car: ~0.17 kg CO₂ saved
      • Train 1 km vs flight: ~0.09 kg CO₂ saved
      • Vegan meal vs beef: ~2.5 kg CO₂ saved
      • Recycling 1 kg plastic: ~1.8 kg CO₂ saved
      • 1°C thermostat reduction (day): ~0.6 kg CO₂ saved
      • Secondhand item vs new: ~5 kg CO₂ saved (avg)

      Sources: IPCC, DEFRA 2023, Project Drawdown

      🤝 Why Community Matters

      The science of social sustainability

      Studies show that social norm visibility increases sustainable behaviour by 20–35% (Goldstein et al., 2008; Nolan et al., 2021). Seeing others act sustainably in your community is the single most effective non-financial lever for behaviour change — more than guilt, fear, or financial incentives alone.

      CommonGround turns your neighbourhood into a positive feedback loop — every logged action reinforces the norm.

      🔒 Privacy

      Your data stays local to your browser

      All your personal actions are stored only in your browser's localStorage. Community data shown here is illustrative/simulated. In a production deployment, individual data would be anonymised and aggregated server-side before display — no personal details are shared with other community members.
